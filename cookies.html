Cookies Policy

This site sets no analytics or advertising cookies. Here is the detail.

Last updated: 23 July 2026

The short version

This website does not set any advertising or analytics cookies. We do not run tracking pixels, and there is no cookie banner because there is nothing here that requires your consent.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website can store on your device, usually to remember something between pages or visits. Similar technologies such as local storage do much the same job.

What this site actually uses

Cedar Hollow's website is a set of static pages. The search on our property listings runs entirely in your browser — your search terms are held in the page address and are not stored or sent to us.

Some parts of the site load fonts and scripts from third-party content delivery networks so pages render quickly. Those requests reach the provider's servers and can be logged by them, which is a normal part of how the web works, but they are not used by us to identify you.

When you leave our site

Our booking links and social media icons take you to other companies' websites — booking partners, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Tripadvisor and Facebook. Once you arrive there, their own cookie and privacy policies apply. We have no control over what they set.

Managing cookies

Every major browser lets you see and delete stored cookies and block new ones, usually under privacy or site settings. Blocking cookies will not stop this website working.

If this changes

If we later add analytics, we will update this page first and introduce a consent prompt if the law requires one. Any questions in the meantime, email hello@cedarhollow.uk.

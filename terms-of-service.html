Staying with us

Booking and payment

  • Full payment is taken at the time of booking.
  • Prices are in pounds sterling and include VAT.
  • Bookings are not transferable unless we agree otherwise in writing.

Cancellations and changes

  • Cancel 30 or more days before check-in for a full refund, less a 5% payment provider fee.
  • Cancel 7 days before check-in for a 75% refund.
  • No refunds for late cancellations, no-shows or early departures.
  • Changing your dates is free. If you move to a different season, you pay the difference.

Check-in and check-out

  • Check-in from 3:00pm. You are welcome to arrive from 1:00pm and enjoy the grounds.
  • Check-out by 11:00am.

Guest responsibilities

  • Please follow the house rules at the retreat.
  • The lead guest must be 21 or over and is responsible for the group.
  • Do not exceed the maximum number of guests for your stay.
  • No parties, no loud music and no fireworks.
  • No smoking anywhere on site.
  • Be kind to the nature around you.
  • Damage or missing items will be charged for.
  • Use of fireworks or Chinese lanterns forfeits your deposit.

Noise

These are quiet woodlands and sound carries further than you would expect. Boomboxes and external speakers are not allowed.

Smoking

Our sites are completely smoke-free. Smoking outdoors is charged at £50 per incident, and smoking indoors carries a £200 cleaning fee. Thank you for helping us protect this place.

Pets

Cedar Hollow Oxford is home to five much-loved cats, so we cannot welcome dogs there. We hope you understand.

Maximum stay

As a condition of our planning permission, bookings at Cedar Hollow Oxford are limited to a maximum of seven nights.

Hot tubs and saunas

  • Use them at your own risk, and take particular care with children. We do not recommend them for under-12s, though we leave that to your judgment.
  • Please shower first and remove make-up.
  • Water left dirty attracts a £100 deep clean charge from your deposit.

Pool, tennis and grounds at Cedar Hollow Oxford

  • Children's swimming times are pre-booked in one-hour slots between 09:00 and 21:00.
  • There are no lifeguards on duty, so please swim safely.
  • The pool bathroom is shared with guests staying in Beaver's Den and Spare 'Oom.
  • Lawn and table tennis equipment is in the box by the court, and court slots are booked in advance.

Accessibility

We believe the woods should be for everyone. Our flagship treehouse at Cedar Hollow Oxford is wheelchair accessible by a woodland ramp. It remains a natural site, so please get in touch about any specific needs and we will tell you honestly what is possible.

Diversity and inclusion

Everyone is welcome here. We embrace all backgrounds, identities and abilities, without judgment or prejudice.

Experiences run by other people

Massages, horse riding, private chefs and similar experiences are booked separately and run by their own providers. They set their own cancellation policies, so please check directly with them.

Forgotten property

We are happy to return anything you leave behind. There is a £14 handling fee, which goes to our cleaner, plus postage.

Before you book

Frequently Asked Questions

The things people email us about most, answered once. If yours is not here, ask us directly and we will add it.

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Where are you?

Two woodlands, both in southern England. Cedar Hollow Oxford is home to The Oaks, and Cedar Hollow Dorset is home to the Woodsman's Treehouse. The map on our homepage shows both, and each listing on the properties page names where it sits.

How do I book?

Each retreat keeps its own calendar, so booking happens on that retreat's own site rather than here. Check availability for The Oaks in Oxfordshire or the Woodsman's Treehouse in Dorset. Payment, confirmation and any changes to your stay are handled there.

What is included?

Every stay is self-contained: a whole building to yourselves, not a room in something larger. Beyond that the retreats differ, and the differences are rather the point. Hot tubs under the canopy, a cold plunge, wood-fired heat, and a deck positioned for the evening sky rather than the car park.

What each one offers is set out on its property page, and the things we run alongside a stay, such as bat walks at dusk and honey from our own hives, are on the experiences page.

Are dogs welcome?

It varies by retreat, so we would rather not give a blanket answer. Check the property page for the stay you have in mind, or ask us before you book and we will tell you plainly.

When can we arrive and leave?

Arrival and departure times are set by each retreat and will be on your booking confirmation. If you are running late, tell the retreat you booked with rather than us: they hold the keys and the schedule.

How accessible are the retreats?

These are buildings in working woodland. Approaches are unsurfaced, some of the accommodation is raised, and a few of the best parts of a stay involve steps. We would rather talk it through than have you arrive to a surprise, so please email us with what you need and we will say honestly which retreat suits and which does not.

What if we need to cancel?

Cancellations follow the terms of the retreat you booked with, since that is who took the payment. Contact them first. The terms covering this website are in our Terms of Service.

Is there phone signal and wi-fi?

Signal is patchy in both woodlands, which most guests come to regard as a feature by the second evening. Where a retreat provides wi-fi it says so on its property page. It is worth downloading your directions before you set off.

Still wondering?

Ask us anything, including the awkward questions. A person reads these, usually the same day.
